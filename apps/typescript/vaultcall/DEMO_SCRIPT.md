## 🎬 Pre-Recording Checklist (10 Seconds)
1. Ensure the app is running on `http://localhost:3000` (`npm run start` or `npm run dev`).
2. Have your browser full screen (1080p or 1440p) with dark mode active.
3. Keep the 3 top tabs ready: **Showcase**, **Live Phone Lab**, and **Treasury Console**.
4. Audio setup: Clear microphone, upbeat confident enterprise cybersecurity tone.

## 🏆 Key Video Production Tips
1. **Pacing**: Speak at a clear, energetic, confident pace (~140 words per minute).
2. **Cursor Motion**: Use smooth, intentional mouse movements. Circle or hover over key indicators (the audio waveform, the stripped burner phone badge, the SHA-256 fingerprint).
3. **Soundtrack**: Use a subtle, low-volume modern tech/synth background track (similar to CareLoop AI) to give that high-production-value feel.
4. **Resolution**: Render at 1080p60 or 4K with clean audio compression.
